## Available Applications & Permits
The Village of Blanchester provides several application forms on its website:

- **Village of Blanchester Application** — for various municipal purposes
- **Application for Parade or Assemblage** — for community events and
  gatherings
- **Curbside Recycling Survey** — for community feedback on recycling
- **Village of Blanchester Final OEPA-SL-Inventory** — venue licensing
  inventory (spreadsheet)
