## Senior Services Available in Blanchester
- **Blanchester Senior Villas** — 344 Pansy Pike, (937) 783-2386
  - 55+ affordable senior housing (40 units)
  - Community center with gym, kitchen, TV, computers
  - Medical alert systems
- **The Laurels of Blanchester** — 839 East Cherry St, (937) 783-4911
  - 50-bed skilled nursing & rehabilitation

- **Continental Manor Nursing & Rehabilitation** — 820 East Center St,
  (937) 783-4949
  - Skilled nursing, rehab, long-term care, respite, hospice
- **Blanchester Community Service Association (EMS/Life Squad)** — PO Box 215

- **Clinton County Community Action — Senior Transportation** — (937) 655-2158
- **Clinton County Elderly Services Program** — (800) 252-0155
