## Overview
Blanchester Community Park is a community park located in the village of
Blanchester, providing recreational space for residents and visitors.

## Key Details
- **Location:** 406 Park Street, Blanchester, OH 45107
- **Phone:** (937) 783-4702
- **Amenities:**
  - Playgrounds
  - Sports fields
  - Picnic shelters
  - Walking paths

## Notes
The park is part of the village's parks and recreation system, which is managed
by the Village of Blanchester. The village website also maintains a Parks and
Recreation events page and a Facebook page for community updates.
