## Overview
The Clinton County Homeless Shelter provides emergency shelter and food
assistance for individuals and families facing homelessness or food
insecurity in Clinton County.

## Key Details
- **Location:** 390 West Main Street, Wilmington, OH 45177
- **Phone:** (937) 382-7058
- **Services:** Food assistance for individuals facing homelessness or

## Notes
The Clinton County Homeless Shelter provides emergency shelter and food
assistance for up to 38 men, women, and children each night across multiple
properties. The shelter serves Blanchester residents and the broader Clinton
County community.
