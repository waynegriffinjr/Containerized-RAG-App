## Overview
Continental Manor Nursing & Rehabilitation Center is a skilled nursing and
rehabilitation facility providing long-term care, short-term sub-acute
rehabilitation, and related services. It is part of the Deaconess Healthcare
network.

## Services
- **Skilled nursing care** (RN and LPN staff)
- **Short-term rehabilitation**
- **Long-term nursing care**
- **Respite care**
- **Hospice care**
- **Physical therapy**
- **Occupational therapy**
- **Speech therapy**
- **Medication management**
- **24/7 nursing staff**
- **Podiatry services**
- **Dental care**
- **Vision care**
- **Palliative care**
- **Wound care**
- **Dementia support groups**

## Key Details
- **Type:** Skilled nursing and rehabilitation center
- **Staffing:** Registered Nurses (RNs), Licensed Practical Nurses (LPNs),
  State Tested Nursing Assistants (STNAs)
- **Setting:** Serene park-like environment
- **Location:** 820 East Center Street, Blanchester, OH 45107
- **Phone:** (937) 783-4949
- **Toll-Free:** (866) 204-9238 (for pricing and availability)

## Additional Services
- **Transportation services** for guests with qualifying follow-up medical
  appointments (no additional charge)
- **Vaccinations** — COVID, flu, pneumonia, RSV offered to guests
- **Discharge planning** — Coordination with home care, DME, and supportive
  services

## Notes
Continental Manor is the other major skilled nursing facility in Blanchester,
along with The Laurels of Blanchester. Both facilities provide comprehensive
skilled nursing and rehabilitation services to the Blanchester community.
