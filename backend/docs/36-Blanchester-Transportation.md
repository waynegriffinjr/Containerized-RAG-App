# Blanchester Transportation Resources

**Source file:** `36-Blanchester-Transportation.md`  
**Source:** Clinton County Resource Directory 2024 (Reach For Tomorrow) — co.clinton.oh.us  
**Location:** Clinton County, including Blanchester

## Transportation Services Available to Blanchester Residents
- **Wilmington Transit System (WTS)** — 69 N South St, Wilmington,
  (937) 382-7961 — Public transportation within Wilmington and surrounding
  areas, curb-to-curb service
- **Clinton County Community Action Program – Senior Transportation** —
  789 N Nelson Ave, Wilmington, (937) 655-2158 — For Clinton County residents
  aged 60+ (medical appointments, grocery shopping, essential errands)
- **Clinton County Elderly Services Program (ESP)** — (800) 252-0155 —
  Transportation assistance for residents 65+
- **Clinton County Community Action Program – Medical Transportation** —
  (937) 382-8365 — Medicaid non-emergency medical transportation

## Notes
Public transportation in Blanchester is limited. The Wilmington Transit
System serves the nearby city of Wilmington and surrounding areas. For
residents of Blanchester, the primary transportation resources are:
1. Private vehicle
2. Senior transportation services (60+) through Community Action
3. Medical transportation (Medicaid) for eligible residents
4. Rides through friends, family, or community volunteers

## Alternative Transportation
For residents without reliable transportation, options include:
- Community Action senior transportation (60+)
- Medical transportation (Medicaid recipients)
- County-wide Blue Bus (Clinton Transit) with on-demand options
- Personal assistance networks

## Notes
Transportation is a key access barrier for Blanchester residents, especially
seniors and those without cars. The Clinton County Community Action Program
and related organizations provide some transportation assistance for eligible
residents.
