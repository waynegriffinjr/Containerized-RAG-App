## Overview
HealthSource of Ohio operates a school-based health center at the Blanchester
schools, providing healthcare services to students and families in the
Blanchester Local School District.

- **Type:** School-based health center / family practice

## Services

- School-based healthcare for students
- Family practice services

## Notes
This health center provides accessible healthcare to students and families
serving the Blanchester school community. It is co-located with the schools,
making it convenient for families in the Blanchester Local School District.
