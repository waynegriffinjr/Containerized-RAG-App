## Community Giving in Blanchester (Fall 2026)
The Vallee Fund for Blanchester, administered in partnership with the Clinton
County Foundation, awarded over $94,000 in fall 2026 to five community projects:

| Recipient | Amount | Project |
|---|---|---|
| Blanchester Local Schools | $25,000 | Student enrichment programs |
| Blanchester Church of Christ | $19,000 | Commercial playground construction |
| Grace United Methodist Church | $19,000 | Stained-glass window preservation |
| Blanchester Middle School PTO | $16,546 | Middle school gym improvements |
| Blanchester Community Service Association | $14,978.74 | EMS equipment update |

**Total:** $94,524.74
