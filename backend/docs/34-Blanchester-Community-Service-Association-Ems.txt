## Overview
The Blanchester Community Service Association (BCSA) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit
that provides emergency rescue services, CPR classes, and community safety
programs to Blanchester and neighboring communities. The organization
operates the **Blanchester Life Squad**, a volunteer-based emergency medical
services (EMS) provider.

## Community Role
The BCSA is a critical community resource, providing emergency medical
transportation and safety education. The Vallee Fund for Blanchester awarded
$14,978.74 to the organization in fall 2026 specifically to update EMS
equipment.
