## Overview
The Blanchester Local School District is the public school district serving the
village of Blanchester and surrounding communities. The district operates
Blanchester High School, Blanchester Middle School, and Putman Elementary
School.

## Notes
The Blanchester Local School District is a key educational institution in the
village, serving students from preschool through 12th grade. The district also
hosts the HealthSource school-based health center and offers athletic programs
through the Blanchester High School.
