## Overview
The Clinton County Community Action Program, Inc. (CCCAP) is a nonprofit
organization providing a wide range of community services, including food
pantries, senior services, housing assistance, weatherization, and emergency
support. The program serves Clinton County residents, including those in
Blanchester.

## Services Offered
- **Food Pantry** — Provides a three-day supply of food to eligible households
  once per month
- **Senior Services** — Supportive services, transportation, housing assistance
- **Employment & Education** — Job training, education programs
- **Housing Programs** — Senior housing management, weatherization
- **Community Center** — Accepting applications for programs

## Notes
The Clinton County Community Action Program is a key resource for low-income
families, seniors, and individuals in need of community assistance. The program
manages senior housing, provides food assistance, and offers a wide range of
support services.

Note: The programs listed above are based in Wilmington and serve the whole
county, including Blanchester. Some services (like senior housing) are
available within Blanchester itself.
