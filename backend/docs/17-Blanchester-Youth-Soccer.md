## Overview
The Blanchester Youth Soccer Organization (BYSO) is a youth sports organization
providing soccer programs for children and teens in the Blanchester community.

## Notes
The BYSO is a member of the Midwest Youth Soccer Organization (MYSO) and
operates across several local communities. The organization is listed in the
townview directory of Blanchester nonprofits as a "Charitable Organization
