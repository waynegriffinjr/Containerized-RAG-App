Blanchester Community Recycling
=================================

Source: Village of Blanchester official website (blanvillage.com); Utah
Department of Environmental Quality (recycling programs)
Location: Blanchester, OH 45107

## Recycling Information
The village of Blanchester offers curbside recycling to residents. The
village website provides information on recycling programs and schedules.

## Key Details
- **Curbside Recycling:** Available to Blanchester residents
- **Recycling Survey:** Available on the village website
- **Clinton County Recycling and Litter Prevention:** 1850 Davids Drive,
  Suite 109, Wilmington, OH 45177, (937) 382-6177

## Services
- Curbside recycling for village residents
- Recycling education and information
- Community recycling programs

## Notes
Recycling is an important community service provided by the Village of
Blanchester. Residents can access curbside recycling through the village
system. For more information, contact the village or the Clinton County
Recycling and Litter Prevention program.
