## Notes
The Village of Blanchester provides a range of utility services including
electric, water, sewer, trash, and gas to village residents. Natural gas
service is provided by Duke Energy Ohio, which is separate from village
utilities. Rumpke provides trash collection on Mondays. The village website
also hosts important utility documents such as the water quality report and
backflow educational materials.
