## Overview
The Blanchester Athletic Boosters is a nonprofit organization that supports
athletic programs in the Blanchester community, primarily through fundraising
and financial support of local school sports.

## Key Details
- **Type:** Charitable organization (fundraising and/or fund distribution)
- **Location:** 953 Cherry Street, Blanchester, OH 45107
- **Established:** 2024
- **Status:** Charitable organization per taxexemptworld

## Services & Activities
- Fundraising for school athletic programs
- Financial support for students and athletics
- Community support for local sports teams

## Notes
The Athletic Boosters work closely with the Blanchester Local School District
to support the Blanchester High School Wildcats and other athletic programs.
The organization is a key community partner in youth sports in the village.
