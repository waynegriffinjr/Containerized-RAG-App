## Overview
The Blanchester Public Library is the primary public library serving the village
of Blanchester and surrounding Clinton County residents. It provides books,
media, programs, and community space for residents of all ages.

## Collection (per village website)
- **50,000+ items** in the collection
- **11,000+ registered cardholders**
- Open-source circulation system

## Programs & Services (as listed on the library website)
- **Crafty Creations** — Adult craft program (monthly, free with supplies provided)
- **Homeschool Hangout** — STEAM activities, first Friday of each month at 2pm (ages 5+)
- **Adult Take-and-Make** — Free craft kits (e.g., cheesecloth ghosts)
- **Reading Dragons** — Trading-card-style reading challenge for children/teens under 18 (school year)
- **Booklovers Discussion** — Monthly book club (e.g., "Remarkably Bright Creatures")
- **Teen Space** — Hangout/study/social space (13–17), first and third Tuesdays of each month
- **Brick Builders** — Lego-building program (third Thursday of each month, ages 5+)
- **Morning Storytime** — Regular storytimes for ages 2–5, plus Family Storytime (Mondays 5–6pm)

## Notes
The Blanchester Public Library is a member of the Consortium of Ohio Libraries
(COOL) and uses the COOL catalog system. Library cards are generally available
to area residents. The library is a vital community hub for education, literacy,
and social connection in Blanchester.
