### Note on Towns Served
District #8 covers Blanchester and Marion Township. The village of Blanchester
also has its own fire/EMS resources, while neighboring villages (Wilmington,
Sabina, Clarksville) have their own separate fire/EMS districts. For residents
of Blanchester, the District #8 Blanchester-Marion Fire Department and Blanchester
EMS are the primary in-village fire/EMS resources.

Note: If calling from outside Blanchester, be sure to state your specific
community so the correct district is dispatched.
