# Blanchester Senior Villas — Senior Housing & Community

**Source file:** `7-Blanchester-Senior-Villas.md`

**Location:** 344 Pansy Pike, Blanchester, OH 45107  
**Phone:** (937) 783-2386

## Overview
Blanchester Senior Villas is an affordable senior housing community managed by
the Clinton County Community Action Program for residents aged 55 and older.

## Key Details
- **Type:** Senior living community, 40-unit apartment complex
- **Floor Plan:** 2-bedroom units with garages
- **Community Amenities:** Kitchen, TV, computers, exercise room, community center
- **Special Features:** Medical alert systems available
- **Phone:** (937) 783-2386

## Services & Features
- Affordable senior housing with well-maintained units
- On-site community center with gathering spaces
- Medical alert systems for resident safety
- Senior-focused amenities (exercise room, community spaces)
