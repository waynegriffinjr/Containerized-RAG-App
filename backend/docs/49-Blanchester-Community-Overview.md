# Blanchester, Ohio — Community Resource Overview

**Source file:** `49-Blanchester-Community-Overview.md`  
**Sources:** Multiple sources as cited in each document; Village of Blanchester

## Overview of Blanchester Community Resources
Blanchester is a village in Clinton County, Ohio, with a diverse set of
community resources supporting residents in government, health, food,
housing, senior services, youth recreation, education, mental health, and
faith-based services.

## Key Resources Summary
- **Government:** Village of Blanchester (318 E Main St, 937-783-2141)
- **Public Safety:** Police, Fire & EMS (District #8), Life Squad EMS
- **Health:** Medical services, school-based health, nursing facilities
- **Food:** Community Food Pantry, Church of Christ pantry, Catholic Charities
- **Housing:** Senior Villas, apartments, community action housing
- **Senior Services:** Community Action, Council on Aging, senior housing
- **Youth:** Sports leagues, library programs, youth outreach
- **Education:** Local school district, public library
- **Mental Health:** Blanchester Counseling Center
- **Faith:** Multiple churches and faith-based organizations
- **Utilities:** Village water, electric, sewer, trash, gas
- **Transportation:** Senior transportation, medical transportation

## Key Community Events & Programs
- **Library programs:** Homeschool Hangout, Teen Space, Reading Dragons
- **Village events:** Parks & Recreation events, village Facebook page
- **Vallee Fund:** Community grants for schools, churches, and services
- **Food drives:** Church and community charitable initiatives

## Service Areas
- Resources in Blanchester village: police, fire/EMS, library, school district,
  parks, community food pantry, senior villas, medical services, churches
- County-wide resources (based in Wilmington): Community Action, Council on
  Aging, homeless shelter, hospitals, food pantries
