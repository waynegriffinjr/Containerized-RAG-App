## Overview
The Blanchester Community Food Pantry is also located at the Blanchester
Church of Christ on Cherry Street, providing food assistance to residents in
need.

## Key Details
- **Location:** Blanchester Church of Christ, 911 Cherry Street, Blanchester, OH 45107
- **Phone:** (937) 783-3504
- **Services:** Provides food assistance to residents in need

## Food Pantry Operating Hours
- **Monday & Thursday:** 6:00 PM – 7:00 PM
- **Note:** Bring ID or a piece of mail; food is available for Blanchester
  School District residents

programs in the village, including the Community Food Pantry, Catholic
Charities mobile pantry, and other church-based charitable services.
