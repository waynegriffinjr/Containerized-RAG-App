## Overview
Your Father's Kitchen, also known as Sugartree Ministries, is a nonprofit
organization that provides hot meals and food pantry services to community
members in need.

## Key Details
- **Location:** 180 E Main Street, Wilmington, OH 45177
- **Phone:** (937) 382-8359
- **Services:** Hot meals and food pantry operations
- **Note:** Call before bringing donations (per organization description)

## Services
- **Hot meal program** — providing meals to community members in need
- **Food pantry** — providing food assistance
- **Community outreach** — supporting families and individuals facing hunger

## Notes
Your Father's Kitchen is based in Wilmington but serves the broader Clinton
County community, including Blanchester residents. The organization provides
hot meals and food pantry access to individuals and families in need.
