## Overview
The Laurels of Blanchester is a 50-bed skilled nursing and rehabilitation
center offering short-term rehabilitation, long-term nursing care, and related
services. It is rated 4-Star in Quality Measures by the Centers for Medicare &
Medicaid Services (CMS).

## Services
- **Skilled nursing care** (long-term)
- **Short-term rehabilitation** (post-hospital care)
- **Sub-acute rehabilitative services**
- **Cardiac rehabilitation**
- **Comprehensive rehabilitation** (physical, occupational, speech therapy)
- **Dental, vision, and podiatry services**
- **Pharmacy services**
- **Laboratory services**
- **IV therapy**
- **Palliative care**
- **Hospice care**
- **Wound care**
- **Dementia support groups**
- **Rehabilitation services**
