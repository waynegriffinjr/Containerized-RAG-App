## Overview
The Council on Aging of Southwestern Ohio (COA) is a nonprofit dedicated to
enhancing the quality of life for older adults, people with disabilities, their
families, and caregivers.

## Services Offered
- **Information and assistance** on programs, services, and resources for
  older adults and people with disabilities
- **Caregiving support**
- **Independent living resources**
- **Supportive services coordination**

## Notes
The Council on Aging and Clinton County Community Action Program provide
comprehensive senior services, including transportation, meal programs,
supportive services, and housing assistance. Blanchester residents should use
the Wilmington-based offices for county-wide services.
