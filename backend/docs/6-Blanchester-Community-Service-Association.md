## Overview
The Blanchester Community Service Association Inc. is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit
organization that provides emergency rescue services, CPR classes, and community
safety services to residents of Blanchester and neighboring communities.
The organization operates the **Blanchester Life Squad**, a volunteer-based
emergency medical services provider.
