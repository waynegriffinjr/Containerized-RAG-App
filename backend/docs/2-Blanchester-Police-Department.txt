Village of Blanchester Police Department
=========================================

Source: blanvillage.com/police-department (Village of Blanchester official website)
Location: 318 E Main Street, Blanchester, OH 45107

Overview
--------
The Blanchester Police Department is a full-service municipal police department
serving the Village of Blanchester. The department is staffed 24 hours a day,
7 days a week, and maintains a visible community policing presence throughout the
village.

Services & Programs:
- 24-hour patrol and response
- Neighborhood Watch Programs
- Crime prevention measures
- Partnerships with Federal, State, and Local law enforcement
- Community policing initiatives
- ALICE training (Active Shooter Response)
- Firearms and Defensive Tactics Instructor certifications
- Records requests and citizen complaint/complaint forms available on the website

Organizational Structure:
- Chief of Police
- Sergeant
- Detective Sergeant
- 5 full-time Police Officers
- Police Administrative Clerk
- 1 part-time police officer
- Ongoing training with instructors, SWAT background, and defensive tactics

Community Engagement:
The department strives to proactively and cooperatively serve the community
with a focus on safety, security, and well-being of all citizens. The mission
is to be "Guardians of life, property and the Constitutional Rights of all
our neighbors."

Notes:
- The Blanchester Police Department is one of several law enforcement agencies
  in Clinton County; dispatch services are contracted through the Clinton
  County Sheriff's Office.
- Blanchester Police is GETS Capable (ESF-13/LE).
- Chief: Scott Reinbolt (per Clinton County Directory).
