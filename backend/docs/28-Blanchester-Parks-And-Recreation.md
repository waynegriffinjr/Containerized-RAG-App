## Overview
The Village of Blanchester Parks and Recreation department manages the village's
parks, recreational facilities, and community events. The department is based
at 933 S Broadway in Blanchester.

## Services
- Management of village parks and recreational facilities
- Community event coordination
- Parks and recreation event scheduling
- Community Facebook page for events ("BlanchesterVillage")

## Key Facilities
- **Blanchester Community Park** — 406 Park Street, (937) 783-4702
  (playgrounds, sports fields, picnic shelters, walking paths)
- **Blanchester Pool & Recreation Center** — 121 E Main St

## Events & Schedules
- Village Events and Parks Events page on the village website
- Parks & Recreation Event Schedule (PDF downloadable from the website)

## Notes
The Parks and Recreation department is a key community resource for
recreational activities, parks maintenance, and community events in Blanchester.
The department works with the Blanchester Community Park, the Pool & Recreation
Center, and other village facilities to provide recreational opportunities for
all residents.
