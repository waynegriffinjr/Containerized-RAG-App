# Catholic Charities Southwestern Ohio — Food Assistance

**Source file:** `30-Catholic-Charities-Southwestern-Ohio.md`  
**Source:** Catholic Charities Southwestern Ohio — `https://www.ccswoh.org/food-assistance/`  
**Location:** Serving Adams, Brown, Clermont, Clinton, Hamilton, and Highland
counties  
**Phone:** (513) 672-3720

## Overview
Catholic Charities Southwestern Ohio provides food assistance through a mobile
pantry program that travels throughout several counties in southwestern Ohio,
including Clinton County. The organization partners with local churches and
organizations to distribute food.

## Food for All Mobile Pantry Program
The mobile pantry is set up at various locations on scheduled dates.

### Blanchester Locations
- **Blanchester Church of Christ** — 911 Cherry St, Blanchester, OH 45107
- **Schedule:** Second Thursday of every even month, 11:00 AM – 1:00 PM

### Other Locations (in Clinton County)
- **St. Mary Catholic Church, Bethel** — 2nd Friday of each month (Clermont
  County)
- **St. Columbkille Catholic Church, Wilmington** — 2nd Thursday of every odd
  month (Wilmington)

## Eligibility & Requirements
- **Eligibility:** TEFAP guidelines based on household size and income level
- **Required:** Picture ID and a current piece of mail for proof of address
- **No paperwork** needed to prove income level
- **Phone for assistance:** (513) 672-3720

## Services
- Food distribution through mobile pantries
- Monthly food distributions at partner churches
- Nutrition education and food access programs

## Notes
Catholic Charities Southwestern Ohio is one of the key food assistance
providers in the region, distributing food through mobile pantries to rural
communities including Blanchester. The organization serves multiple counties
and partners with local churches for distribution.
